# Activity 6: AI Website Development

## Project: NAIRA – Bags and Accessories

### About the Project

This repository contains the output for **Activity 6: AI Website Development**, which explores the use of artificial intelligence tools in creating and developing a business website.

For this activity, I conceptualized **NAIRA**, a contemporary fashion accessories brand offering stylish, functional, and versatile bags and everyday essentials. Using AI-assisted website development, I transformed this business concept into a visually appealing online storefront.

## Business Concept

**Brand Name:** NAIRA  
**Tagline:** "Everyday essentials, thoughtfully styled."  
**Business Type:** Fashion Bags and Lifestyle Accessories

NAIRA is a proposed fashion accessories brand that offers thoughtfully designed products suitable for everyday use. The brand focuses on combining functionality, versatility, and timeless style.

Its target market includes college students, young professionals, working women, and fashion-conscious individuals who appreciate practical yet aesthetically pleasing accessories.

## Website Objectives

The website serves as a digital storefront for NAIRA, designed to:

- Showcase different bag styles and accessory collections.
- Present product designs, available colors, and prices.
- Provide an organized and visually appealing shopping experience.
- Establish a recognizable brand identity.
- Make product browsing convenient and accessible for potential customers.

## Product Categories

The website features the following product categories:

- Tote Bags
- Shoulder Bags
- Baguette Bags
- Crossbody Bags
- Laptop Bags and Cases
- Wallets and Accessories

## Website Features

The developed website incorporates several sections to present the brand and its products:

- **Homepage:** Introduces the brand through its visual identity, tagline, and featured products.
- **Bestsellers & New Arrivals:** Showcases popular products and newly introduced designs.
- **Product Categories:** Organizes products according to their respective styles and functions.
- **Shop by Collection:** Features curated collections such as The Everyday Edit, The Workroom, and The City Edit.
- **About Us:** Introduces the brand's identity, philosophy, and vision.
- **Product Showcase:** Displays product images, prices, and available color variations.

## Design Concept

The website follows a minimalist and contemporary aesthetic, emphasizing:

- Neutral and earthy color palettes.
- Beige, cream, brown, and other complementary tones.
- Clean layouts and elegant typography.
- High-quality product imagery.
- A sophisticated yet welcoming shopping atmosphere.

The overall design reflects NAIRA's identity as a brand that values both functionality and timeless aesthetics.

## AI Tools Used

- **Replit**
- **Base44**

These AI-assisted tools were explored to help translate the business concept into a functional website through text-based prompts and additional instructions.
