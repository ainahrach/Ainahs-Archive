# From Response to Connection: Exploring Google Forms & Google Meet

## About the Project

This repository contains an educational presentation prepared by **Group 2** exploring two essential digital tools under Google Workspace: **Google Forms** and **Google Meet**.

The presentation introduces the purpose, features, and functions of both applications, highlighting how they support digital communication, collaboration, information gathering, and online learning.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Google Forms

Google Forms is a web-based application that allows users to create digital forms, surveys, questionnaires, quizzes, and registration forms while collecting responses electronically.

**Topics discussed:**
- Definition and purpose of Google Forms
- Data collection and management
- Surveys, questionnaires, and feedback collection
- Online quizzes and assessments
- Creating and customizing forms
- Different question types:
  - Multiple Choice
  - Short Answer
  - Paragraph
  - Checkboxes
  - Dropdown
  - Linear Scale
  - Rating
  - File Upload
  - Multiple Choice Grid
  - Checkbox Grid
  - Date and Time
- Preview Mode
- Managing responses and settings
- Additional features and functions

### 2. Google Meet

Google Meet is a video communication platform that allows users to conduct online meetings, communicate, and collaborate using their phones, tablets, or computers.

**Topics discussed:**
- Definition and purpose of Google Meet
- Virtual meetings and video/audio calls
- Screen sharing and presentations
- Integration with Google Workspace
- Video and audio features
- Noise cancellation and visual effects
- Collaboration and engagement tools
- Interactive whiteboards
- Breakout rooms
- Live polling, Q&A, and Raise Hand features
- Live captions and host controls

## Learning Objectives

By exploring this presentation, learners are expected to:

1. Understand the purpose and functions of Google Forms and Google Meet.
2. Identify the different features and tools available in both applications.
3. Explore how Google Forms can be utilized for information gathering, assessments, and feedback collection.
4. Understand how Google Meet facilitates virtual communication and collaboration.
5. Recognize the importance of digital tools in modern education and communication.
