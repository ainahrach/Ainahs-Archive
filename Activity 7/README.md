# Activity 7: Resume Generating Using AI Tools

## About the Activity

This repository contains the output for **Activity 7: Resume Generating**, which explores the capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) in creating professional documents, particularly résumés for job applications.

For this activity, two AI tools, **Claude AI and ChatGPT**, were utilized to generate a professional nursing résumé using the same set of information and instructions. Their outputs were then compared based on content generation, organization, language, conciseness, and overall presentation.

## Objectives

This activity aims to:

- Explore how AI tools can assist in creating professional résumés.
- Identify how LLMs organize and present educational backgrounds, clinical experiences, and professional qualifications.
- Compare the outputs of different AI tools using identical prompts.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of AI-generated documents in professional applications.
- Recognize the importance of human review in ensuring the accuracy and relevance of AI-generated content.

## AI Tools Used

### 1. Claude AI
Used to generate a professional nursing résumé based on the provided personal information, educational background, and clinical experiences.

### 2. ChatGPT
Used to generate an alternative résumé, emphasizing clinical competencies, professional strengths, adaptability, and leadership experience.

## Résumé Contents

The generated résumés included the following sections:

- Professional Summary
- Clinical Experience and Training
- Clinical and Technical Skills
- Educational Background
- Affiliated Organizations
- Professional Strengths

## Comparative Analysis

The two AI-generated résumés were evaluated using the following criteria:

| Criteria | Claude AI | ChatGPT |
|---|---|---|
| Content Generation | Emphasized the applicant's clinical background. | Highlighted clinical experience, competencies, education, and professional strengths. |
| Professional Summary | Presented qualifications in a formal manner. | Emphasized adaptability, leadership, and patient-centered care. |
| Organization | Used a structured résumé format. | Organized information into clearly defined sections. |
| Language and Tone | Maintained a formal and professional tone. | Used professional but natural-sounding language. |
| Conciseness | Condensed information to fit the requested format. | Used short competency phrases to minimize repetition. |
| Presentation | Provided a professional résumé layout. | Generated a downloadable one-page résumé in Word and PDF formats. |

Both AI tools were helpful in producing professional résumés. However, ChatGPT provided a more personalized presentation of the applicant's experiences, while Claude AI offered a structured and formal approach.

## Key Takeaways

Through this activity, I learned that:

- AI tools can simplify the process of creating professional documents.
- Different LLMs may produce varying results even when given identical prompts.
- Effective prompt writing influences the quality and relevance of generated content.
- AI-generated résumés still require human review to ensure accuracy and authenticity.
- Personal judgment remains essential in presenting one's qualifications and experiences appropriately.
